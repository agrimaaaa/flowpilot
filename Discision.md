1. Why this ingestion strategy over the obvious alternative I rejected?
I chose a source-aware ingestion strategy that can handle failures and changing source conditions instead of relying on a single straightforward request flow. The obvious alternative was a simple one-shot ingestion approach, but it is more fragile if a source blocks requests or changes its behavior. My approach gives the pipeline more control over retries, validation, and failure handling.
2. One trade-off you made under the time limit, and what you’d do with a real week.
I focused on getting the ingestion flow and the homepage working well for FlowPilot instead of trying to cover every possible issue. If I had a week to work on FlowPilot I would add better monitoring improve how FlowPilot recovers from failures do more testing with different sources and make sure the validation is strong enough for production
